SOP-OVPAA-IEO-01Submission of Required Documents

Required documents

After a referral, the student brings these documents to the Inclusive Education Office. Do not upload them online. The office checks the originals or copies in person.

The process may not push forward without the required document. The needs assessment interview is scheduled only after the required documents are complete.

Neurodivergent conditions, and physical or sensory impairments

Required: a clinical diagnosis from a physician, psychiatrist, or psychologist.

Bring it together with other supporting documents pertinent to your condition. These may include, but are not limited to:

  • PWD Identification Card
  • Individualized Education Plan from previous school, if available
  • Progress Reports from occupational therapists, physical therapists, speech and language therapists, and other multidisciplinary team providers

No clinical diagnosis yet? Submit a Request for Referral Form for Clinical Diagnosis (RTU-OVPAA-IEO-F004). The IEO prepares a Referral Form for Required Documents for your physician, psychiatrist, or psychologist and emails you when it is ready for pickup.

Indigenous People (IPs)

Required: a Certificate of Confirmation (COC) or a Tribal/Community Endorsement.

Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs)

Required: both of these:

  • Endorsement from the BJMP Coordinator
  • Clearance from CAWO

If more than one applies

Bring the documents for every category that applies to you. For example, an IP student with a hearing impairment brings a clinical diagnosis and a COC or Tribal/Community Endorsement.
