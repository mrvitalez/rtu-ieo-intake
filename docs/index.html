Needs Assessment and Student Profiling

The Inclusive Education Office supports RTU students with neurodivergent conditions, physical and learning impairments, and students from marginalized sectors.

A needs assessment informs IEO whether a student is qualified for an Individualized Support Plan (ISP) for a meaningful and inclusive university experience.

If you are concerned about a student, or about yourself, start with the REFERRAL SLIP.

It only takes about five minutes.

Refer a student See required documents

How the process works?

  1. Start of ProcessA referrer notices a student who may need academic accommodation, behavioral intervention, or transition support.
  2. Reason for ReferralThe referrer provides context and reason for referral (e.g. behavioral concerns, academic or transition challenges, etc.)
  3. Submission of Digital Referral SlipThe referrer (e.g. CAWO, the Admissions Office, the University Clinic, a faculty member, a classmate, or the student) submits the Digital Referral Slip (F001).
  4. Submission of Required DocumentsThe referred student submits the necessary documents for their case. Only students with complete documents may proceed to the next step.

    Students with neurodivergent conditions and physical or sensory impairments without clinical diagnosis will be given a Referral Form for Required Documents.
  5. Completion of Student Portfolio FormThe referred student fills in the Student Portfolio Form (F002) sent via the student’s registered email address provided by the referrer.
  6. Needs Assessment InterviewThe student is scheduled for an interview by the IEO-Case Manager.
  7. Educational Assistance Decision
    • Yes: the IEO proceeds with the Formulation of the ISP (SOP-OVPAA-IEO-02).
    • No: the student is asked to sign the waiver section of the Student Portfolio Form.
  8. End of ProcessThe IEO closes the case.

Your privacy

Every form shows the University's Data Privacy Consent under Republic Act 10173. Answers go only to the Inclusive Education Office's records and are seen only by authorized IEO staff. Nobody, including the person who sent a form, can look up a student's record on this website.
