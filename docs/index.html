Needs Assessment and Student Profiling

The Inclusive Education Office supports RTU students with neurodivergent conditions, physical and learning impairments, and students from marginalized sectors.

A needs assessment informs IEO whether a student is qualified for an Individualized Support Plan (ISP) for a meaningful and inclusive university experience.

If you are concerned about a student, or about yourself, start with the REFERRAL SLIP.

It only takes about five minutes.

How the process works?

  1. Referral (public site)
    1. The referrer opens the Referral Slip (F001) at mrvitalez.github.io/rtu-ieo-intake/f001.html.
    2. They fill in the student’s details: name, program (dropdown; department fills in automatically), student no., student email, birthday, sex, the reason for referral, and other observations. Then their own details as referrer, agreement to the Data Privacy Consent, and their typed name as signature.
    3. On Submit, the referrer gets a reference number (for example IEO-2026-0001), a new case is created in the Google Sheet, and IEO staff receive an alert email.
  2. Reason for Referral (staff panel)
    1. Staff opens the case and reviews the reason for referral. Nothing else can move forward until this is saved.
    2. A Case Manager is assigned to contact the student via email.
  3. Follow up for the required documents (staff panel)
    1. Attempt 1: The case manager emails the referred student a template (editable) asking for the required documents. Calls and walk-ins can also be logged.
    2. The system waits 3 working days for a reply.
      • Responded: The case manager clicks “Responded,” the follow up ends, and the case goes to Stage 4.
      • No response: after 3 working days, the case manager proceeds with the next attempt.
    3. Attempt 2: contact the referrer. This is skipped for self referrals.
    4. Attempt 3: contact the department head of the student’s program, from the Programs tab.
    5. If there’s no response after the last attempt, the referral closes automatically and the referrer can be notified. The case manager can reopen it if the student gets in touch later.
  4. Submission of required documents (in person; staff panel)
    1. The student brings the documents to the IEO. Nothing is uploaded online. The case manager chooses the case category and ticks what was received:
      • Neurodivergent / physical or sensory: clinical diagnosis (required), plus supporting documents if available.
      • IP: COC or Tribal/Community Endorsement.
      • PDL: BJMP Coordinator endorsement and CAWO clearance.
    2. If documents are incomplete:
      • IP or PDL: the student is asked to submit the missing documents.
      • No clinical diagnosis yet: the student submits the Request for Referral Form for Clinical Diagnosis (F004) on the public site, using their reference number. Staff then issue the F003 (Referral Form for Required Documents) from the panel. The student is emailed to pick it up at the IEO, takes it to their physician, psychiatrist or psychologist, and brings back the diagnosis.
    3. The process will not push forward without the required documents. The interview button stays locked until they’re complete.
  5. Interview scheduling and Student Portfolio
    1. The case manager clicks “Documents complete: schedule interview” and sets the date, time, and venue (in person or online link).
    2. The student automatically receives:
      • an email with the schedule and a personal link to the Student Portfolio Form (F002),
      • a Google Calendar invite.
    3. The student fills in F002 through the link, before or during the interview. All information input can be saved and edited by the student. The link expires after 30 days.
    4. The Case Manager conducts the needs assessment interview. The case manager records the sessions (Intake, Sessions 1 to 10) and case notes. Interviews can be rescheduled.
  6. Educational assistance decision (staff panel)
    1. The case manager records the result:
      • Yes, needs assistance: proceed with the Formulation of the ISP (SOP-OVPAA-IEO-02).
      • No assistance needed: the ISP waiver section of F002 unlocks, and the student gets a new link to sign the waiver online.
  7. End of process
    1. The case manager clicks Close case. A case can also be closed early with a reason, such as the student’s voluntary withdrawal.

Your privacy

Every form shows the University's Data Privacy Consent under Republic Act 10173. Answers go only to the Inclusive Education Office's records and are seen only by authorized IEO staff. Nobody, including the person who sent a form, can look up a student's record on this website.
