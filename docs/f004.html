For referred students with a neurodivergent condition or a physical or sensory impairment who do not have a clinical diagnosis yet.

Use this form to ask the Inclusive Education Office for a Referral Form for Required Documents addressed to your physician, psychiatrist, or psychologist. The IEO prepares it and emails you when it is ready for pickup at the office.

What happens next

  1. The Inclusive Education Office prepares your Referral Form for Required Documents.
  2. You get an email when it is ready. Pick it up at the IEO and bring your school ID.
  3. Sign the student’s authorization on the form, then bring it to your physician, psychiatrist, or psychologist.
  4. Return the completed form and your clinical diagnosis to the IEO.
